Targeting Rules
IF a vehicle matches the conditions (combined with nested AND/OR), THEN take the action. Actions: Drop on import (never enters SCRAPERDATA), Exclude from CAO (skipped in auto-fill only), Exclude from orders (skipped in CAO and runs; the Bypass checkbox overrides per run). Make/Model are raw text — list keyword variants like F-250 and F250. A rule with no conditions does nothing.

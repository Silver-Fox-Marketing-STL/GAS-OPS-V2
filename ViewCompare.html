Paste two lists of VINs and/or stock #s — one per line, or separated by commas, tabs or spaces. Every entry is looked up first (a stock # becomes its vehicle's VIN), then the lists are split into A only, in both and B only — so a VIN in one list matches its stock # in the other. Hover an ALREADY PRINTED status for the dates of the orders it was printed on.

Paste a list of stock #s (VINs work too) — one per line, or separated by commas, tabs or spaces. Each is looked up in current inventory and returned with its VIN, vehicle data and prior orders. Copy VINs on the Found tab gives you a VIN list ready for Run Order.
